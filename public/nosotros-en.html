01OUR STORY

Duralco was founded on August 30, 2018, with the vision of providing solutions for civil and structural projects.

From the start, it drew on the experience of one of its founders in the metalworking industry, whose career in the sector began in 2011.

Over time, our clients’ needs drove us to expand our capabilities. That is how the divisions that make up Grupo Duralco today came together: Duralco Constructores, Corebolt, Mecatechn, Alidpart and Guacamaya Design.

Each brings its own specialty and shares one purpose: to provide personalized service and solutions that create value.

02OUR TEAM

The people behind every project.

Engineers, technicians, workshop and field staff who handle every project up close, train continuously and keep direct contact with our clients.

03CENTRAL AMERICA

A vision that crosses borders.

In 2026 we began our expansion into Nicaragua, El Salvador and Guatemala, a step that reflects our commitment to building lasting relationships and bringing our solutions to our neighboring Central American countries.

We grow while keeping the close, personalized service on which Duralco was founded.

04OUR PURPOSE

Grupo Duralco is more than a group of companies: it is an integrated service created to give confidence and security to its clients and team members.

We want every client to find a team committed to their project, and every team member to be part of an organization where their work contributes to shared growth.

Mission

To deliver integrated solutions for civil, structural and industrial projects, connecting our specialties, the talent of our team and technology to create value and trust.

Vision

To become a leading business group and a benchmark in Central America, recognized for the quality of our solutions, personalized service and commitment to our clients and team members.
