Grupo Duralco — Engineering, steel structures and industrial manufacturing in San Pedro Sula, Honduras

Business group headquartered in San Pedro Sula, Honduras, integrating engineering, construction, fabrication, industrial supply and design for projects in Honduras, Guatemala, El Salvador and Nicaragua.

Divisions

  • Duralco Constructores: structural engineers and builders. Steel structures, industrial buildings, warehouses, civil works, electromechanical installation, tanks, silos and conveyor belts in Honduras.
  • Corebolt: manufacturing of ASTM F1554 anchor bolts, J, L and straight bolts, Nelson studs, base plates and custom structural anchoring systems.
  • Mecatechn: CNC and conventional machining in San Pedro Sula, reverse engineering and industrial parts manufacturing.
  • Alidpart: industrial spare parts, gear motors, industrial heaters and food industry equipment across Central America.
  • Guacamaya Design: urban furniture, playgrounds, synthetic turf and recreational spaces.
